About Milestone

We rent out 42 cars.
That's the whole business.

No loyalty tiers, no upsell calls, no fine print you need a lawyer for. Just cars that are looked after, and people who answer the phone.

How it started

A garage, a spreadsheet, and one very reliable hatchback

Milestone began in 2017 with a single car and a neighbour who needed it for a wedding. Word got around, the spreadsheet grew into a small fleet, and the garage became an actual office — though we still keep a printed logbook next to the laptop, mostly out of habit.

Nine years on, the fleet has grown to 42 cars across six pickup points, but the way we run things hasn't changed much: every car is inspected between rentals, every price is fixed upfront, and every question gets answered by a person, not a script.

What we believe

Four rules we don't break

  • 01

    The quoted price is the final price

    Insurance, fuel at pickup, and roadside help are already folded into the number you see. Nothing gets added at the counter.

  • 02

    Every car earns its place in the fleet

    Cars are serviced on a fixed schedule and retired once they stop meeting it — no exceptions for a "still runs fine" car.

  • 03

    A real person picks up the phone

    The helpline is staffed by people who can actually make a decision, not a menu that loops back to itself.

  • 04

    Cancel without an argument

    Plans change. Cancel up to 24 hours before pickup and get a full refund, no explanation required.

Booking, in short

Four steps, no paperwork to chase later

Step 1

Pick your car

Browse the fleet and choose what fits the trip — city runabout, sedan, or SUV.

Step 2

Send your dates

Reach out with pickup and return dates. We confirm availability the same day.

Step 3

Show up with a licence

One form, one signature, a quick walkaround of the car together.

Step 4

Drive

Return it with a full tank at the agreed time and spot. That's the whole process.
